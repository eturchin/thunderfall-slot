# Engineering contracts
Keep mathematical execution free of IO, clocks, wallets, and framework dependencies.
Use checked integer/fixed-point settlement, immutable definitions, and version all math changes.
Never truncate cascades on work exhaustion: fail the round and roll back.
A paid cycle owns its PRNG stream, retriggers, cap, and statistical observation.
Test traced/untraced equality, replay, deterministic parallel aggregation, and wallet reconciliation after edits.
Record measured results and environmental limits in PROGRESS.md. Never fabricate verification.
Do not edit the read-only research clones or unrelated repositories.
